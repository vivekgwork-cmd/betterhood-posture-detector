Frequently asked questions

Why does it calibrate to me instead of just knowing good posture? +

Everyone's neutral sitting position is a little different — camera angle, chair height, neck length. Comparing you to your own upright reference is far more reliable than guessing at an absolute "correct" posture from a single frame.

Does my camera feed get uploaded anywhere? +

No. Every frame is analyzed locally using on-device image processing. Nothing is recorded, stored, or sent to a server — closing the tab discards everything.

Where is my reference posture saved? +

Only in this browser's local storage, on this device. It's not synced anywhere. Clearing your browser data or clicking Recalibrate resets it.

Do I need to stay in the exact same spot? +

Try to stay roughly where you calibrated. Leaning in noticeably closer to the camera than your reference is itself one of the things it watches for, so drifting toward the screen will register as a slouch cue.
