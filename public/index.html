    Before you catch anyone

    Is my friend's photo stored anywhere?

    No. The photo is held in memory just long enough to analyse it, then discarded. The Caught Card is drawn on your phone, and only goes where you send it.

    Why do you ask for my phone number?

    Only when you share on WhatsApp, so betterhood knows who's playing posture police. It's never printed on the Caught Card or shared with the person you caught. Don't want to give it? Close the form and you can still download the card.

    Should I ask before snapping someone?

    Keep it friendly. Catch people who'd laugh along, and show them their Caught Card before you share it with a group.

    Can I catch myself?

    Absolutely. Prop your phone up, sit how you normally do, and see which animal you really are.

    Is this a medical diagnosis?

    No, it's for fun and awareness. If you have ongoing pain, please see a physiotherapist.
